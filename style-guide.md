# Little Lemon – Brand Style Guide

This document specifies the official brand identity, typography, color palette, and asset guidelines for the Little Lemon capstone project. All UI/UX implementation must strictly adhere to these specifications.

---

## 1. About the Brand
* [cite_start]**Brand Name:** Little Lemon [cite: 25, 26]
* [cite_start]**Vertical:** Restaurant / Charming neighborhood bistro [cite: 27, 28, 40]
* [cite_start]**Tone & Environment:** Lively but casual, simple, warm, and inviting[cite: 40, 52].

---

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary Colors
* [cite_start]**Primary Green:** `#495E57` (Main brand color used for backgrounds, headers, and major structural elements) [cite: 48]
* **Primary Yellow:** `#F4CE14` (Accent color used for primary actions, highlights, and core branding)

### Secondary & Highlight Colors
* [cite_start]**Light Tint / Background:** `#EDEFEE` [cite: 55]
* [cite_start]**Dark Neutral / Charcoal:** `#333333` (Used for text and high-contrast readability) [cite: 62]

---

## 3. Typography

### Headlines & Titles
* [cite_start]**Font Family:** `Markazi Text` [cite: 31, 33]
* [cite_start]**Weight:** Medium [cite: 32]
* [cite_start]**Casing:** Sentence case [cite: 34]
* [cite_start]**Line Height:** Same point size as the headline (Leading matches font size)[cite: 34].

### Body Text & Paragraphs
* [cite_start]**Font Family:** `Karla` [cite: 42, 43]
* [cite_start]**Weight:** Regular [cite: 42]
* [cite_start]**Size:** 65% of the headline font size [cite: 43]
* [cite_start]**Casing:** Sentence case[cite: 43].

---

## 4. Imagery & Photography Guidelines

### Do's (Approved Usage)
* [cite_start]Use high-quality, authentic photography featuring simple, casual restaurant environments[cite: 51, 60].
* [cite_start]Focus on product and food shots that feel warm, inviting, and shot in natural light[cite: 52, 60].

### Don'ts (Prohibited Usage)
* [cite_start]Avoid studio setups or clinical food photography that feels overly engineered or stylized[cite: 61].
* [cite_start]Do not use overly complex graphic compositions or flat, synthetic top-down asset illustrations[cite: 1].

---

## 5. Logo Variations & Implementation

### Primary Logos (Preferred)
* [cite_start]**Two-Color Version:** Features the custom lemon icon (Yellow/Green) accompanied by the text "LITTLE LEMON" in the brand's primary green[cite: 21].
* [cite_start]**Application:** Always default to the primary two-color logo variations (horizontal wordmark or stacked icon + text) whenever background contrast permits[cite: 7, 21].

### Monochrome Logos (Fallback)
* [cite_start]**Application:** Use only when layout constraints or background colors prevent the primary two-color versions from being legible[cite: 23].
* [cite_start]**Variations:** White monochrome assets on solid green backgrounds, or dark monochrome assets on light backgrounds[cite: 8, 9].
